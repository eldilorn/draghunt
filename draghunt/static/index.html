How Draghunt works

  1. Run an exercise. Draghunt stages an incident and hands you the evidence.
  2. Investigate and write the report. Read the events, cite the ones that matter, and record your findings.
  3. Submit to see the answer key. Your findings are scored, and the debrief shows what actually happened.

Synthetic exercises use fictional events generated on this machine. Nothing touches your lab. When you're ready to run real attacks against your own range, add it under Settings.
